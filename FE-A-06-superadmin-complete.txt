  WHAT WAS BUILT
================================================================================

The platform-admin area (SuperAdmin dashboard + Orgs table) for PLATFORM_ADMIN
(SUPER_ADMIN) role. Lives under /admin/* routes, inside the existing
DashboardLayout, gated by RoleRoute.

Pages:
  /admin             → SuperAdmin (Platform Overview dashboard)
  /admin/orgs        → Orgs (Organisations table with CRUD modals)
  /admin/orgs/:id    → OrgDetail (future — modal-based, wire-ready)
